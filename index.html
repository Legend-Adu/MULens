About MULens
Preserving Metropolitan University Memories

MULens is the digital media archive of Metropolitan University, Sylhet, Bangladesh. It is dedicated to documenting, archiving, and showcasing the vibrant campus life, historic university milestones, academic ceremonies, cultural celebrations, sports triumphs, and everyday moments.

Through collaborative student photojournalism and community submissions, MULens ensures every campus story, perspective, and voice is immortalized for current students, alumni, faculty, and future generations.
